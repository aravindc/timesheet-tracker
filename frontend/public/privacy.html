Privacy Policy

Effective date: March 2026  ·  Last updated: March 2026

Timesheet Tracker ("we", "our", or "us") is a personal productivity tool hosted at timesheet.pravitha.in. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

1. Data We Collect

When you use Timesheet Tracker, we may collect:

  • Account data — username, hashed password (for local accounts), or provider ID and email address (when you sign in via Google or Facebook).
  • Work data — project names, work-day entries, start/end times, break hours, and hourly rate information you enter.
  • Technical data — server logs (IP address, request path, timestamp) retained for up to 30 days for security and debugging purposes.

We do not collect payment information, track you across third-party websites, or use advertising cookies.

2. How We Use Your Data

  • To authenticate you and secure your account.
  • To store and display your timesheet entries and payslip calculations.
  • To diagnose errors and maintain service availability.

3. Social Login (Google & Facebook)

If you choose to sign in via Google or Facebook, we receive your public profile ID and email address from those providers. We use this solely to create or identify your account. We do not access your contacts, posts, or any other data from those platforms.

Please review Google's Privacy Policy and Meta's Privacy Policy for how they handle your data before redirecting to us.

4. Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored in a PostgreSQL database hosted on a third-party cloud provider. Passwords are hashed using bcrypt and never stored in plain text. All connections are encrypted via TLS. We apply reasonable technical measures to protect your data, but no system is completely secure.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties, except as required by law or to operate the service (e.g. the database hosting provider).

6. Data Retention & Deletion

Your account and all associated data are retained for as long as the account exists. To request deletion, contact us at the address below and we will permanently remove your account and data within 30 days.

7. Your Rights

You may request at any time:

  • Access to the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correction of inaccurate data.
  • Deletion of your account and data.
  • Export of your timesheet data.

8. Cookies

We use a single browser localStorage entry to store your authentication token. No third-party cookies or tracking pixels are used.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Continued use of the service after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

10. Contact

Questions about this policy? Reach us at admin@pravitha.in.
